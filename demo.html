The route, in words

  1. Start at the post office. Three letters need delivering. Time moves only when you act.
  2. Take the always-open bridge to Tern House. The first letter is delivered and the tide rises.
  3. At Rising tide, take the ferry to Bell Tower. Two letters delivered, two turns used.
  4. Try Gull Cottage. The causeway is closed at High tide, and the rejected move costs nothing.
  5. Wait one turn. The tide falls and the causeway opens.
  6. Cross to Gull Cottage. The last letter is delivered.
  7. Take the bridge home. The five-turn route becomes a postcard. Download the real SVG keepsake.
  8. Start the next round. Six little maps offer new routes, with unlimited undo and no clock.
