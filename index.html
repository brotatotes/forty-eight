    WELCOME TO THE ISLAND POST

    Take your time.
    The letters can wait.

    1. Deliver three letters. Choose a connected island on the chart or in the route list. Mail is delivered automatically when you arrive.
    2. Read the tide. Bridges are always open. Causeways open at Low and Falling. Ferries run at Rising and High.
    3. Every action turns the tide. A move or a wait advances one phase. Check the tide before crossing. Time never passes by itself.
    4. Come home. Return to the post office with an empty satchel to earn a route postcard.

    There is no timer or wrong turn. Undo as often as you like. Use Tab and Enter to play by keyboard.
